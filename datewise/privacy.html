Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

DateWise ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by WiselyRise. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your information when you use the DateWise mobile application.

1. Information You Provide

1.1 Reminders & Events

When you create reminders, DateWise stores locally on your device: titles, dates, notes, notification times, repeat rules, relationship tags, and reminder types. This data is stored in a local SQLite database protected by your device's built-in security.

1.2 People Hub

If you use the People Hub, we store locally: names, relationship categories (Family, Friends, Work, etc.), phone numbers (optional), email addresses (optional), WhatsApp handles (optional), contact lookup keys (optional), and notes. All People data stays on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup (Pro feature), this data is also backed up to your personal Drive App Data folder.

1.3 Inbox

Notes and capture items you add to the Inbox (title, notes, linked person, category, tags) are stored locally on your device. They are included in Google Drive backup only if you explicitly enable it.

1.4 Contact Import

If you choose to import from your device contacts, we access contact names, birthday/anniversary dates, and optionally profile photos. We request this permission only when you explicitly initiate an import. We read contacts — we never write to or modify your contacts.

1.5 Photos & Avatars

If you attach a photo to a reminder or person profile, it is stored locally on your device. If you enable the optional "Include Photos" setting for Google Drive backup, avatar photos are also uploaded to your personal Google Drive App Data folder. Photos are never shared with third parties or stored on our servers.

1.6 Google Sign-In (Cloud Backup, Pro)

If you sign in for Google Drive backup, we receive your Google display name, email address, and profile photo URL from Google. We use this solely to authenticate you and to identify your backup file in Google Drive App Data. We do not store your Google credentials on our servers.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • App & Device Info: We may collect basic device information (OS version, app version) for crash diagnostics only.
  • We do not collect location data, advertising identifiers, browsing history, or behavioral analytics.
  • Smart Add Trials: The number of Smart Add sessions used (free trial count) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This count is not transmitted to any server.
  • Engagement Signals: Lightweight local counters (e.g. days used, reminders created) are stored on-device only to offer contextual prompts such as upgrade suggestions. No engagement data is transmitted to servers.

3. Data Storage & Security

3.1 Local Storage

All reminder, people, and inbox data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. Your data remains on your device unless you explicitly enable Google Drive backup.

3.2 Deleted Data & Recycle Bin

Reminders you delete are moved to the Recycle Bin and kept locally for up to 30 days before permanent deletion. People you delete are recorded in a local tombstone ledger (used for cloud sync conflict resolution) with entries pruned automatically after 365 days. This tombstone data stays on your device and is included in cloud backup only to support conflict-free sync.

3.3 Google Drive Backup (Pro Feature)

If you enable Google Drive backup, your reminder, people, and inbox data is stored in your personal Google Drive account's App Data folder — accessible only by DateWise and by you. If you additionally enable the "Include Photos" option, avatar photos are also backed up. Google Drive servers comply with industry-standard security practices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3.4 Security Measures

  • Local data is protected by your device's built-in security (lock screen, encryption).
  • Cloud data is stored in your personal Google Drive App Data folder, which is inaccessible to other apps and to WiselyRise servers.
  • App Lock (biometric/fingerprint) provides an additional privacy layer within the app.
  • No user data is ever transmitted to WiselyRise servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. The only external parties involved in data processing are:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Drive: Used for authentication and cloud backup (governed by Google's Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Billing: Used for processing the one-time Pro in-app purchase (governed by Google Play's terms).

Your reminder, people, and inbox data is never transmitted to WiselyRise or any third-party server except your own Google Drive when you opt in.

5. Your Rights

  • Access & Export: You can export all your data at any time using Settings → Local Backup (JSON format).
  • Deletion: Delete individual reminders (moved to Recycle Bin for 30 days), or clear all data from within the app. Google Drive backup data can be deleted using the Delete Backup option in the Cloud Backup screen.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke contacts, notification, or Google Sign-In permissions at any time via your device app settings or your Google account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

DateWise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will promptly address it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
