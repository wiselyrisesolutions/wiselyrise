2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

A. Local Device Storage (Default Standalone Mode)

When you use GrabWise in default Standalone Mode, all data is retained strictly on your device using sandboxed local storage (SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • Grocery items, quantities, measurement units, purchase status flags, and custom aisle notes.
  • Preferred store assignments (e.g., DMart, vegetable market, local Kirana).
  • Recent purchase history used strictly on-device to calculate "Buy Again" recommendations.
  • User interface preferences: app language (English, Tamil, Bilingual), visual style, Elder Mode settings, and auto-cleanup durations.

WiselyRise servers never receive, intercept, or store this local standalone data.

B. Cloud-Shared Household Data (Opt-In Family Synchronization)

When you choose to synchronize grocery lists in real time with family members or flatmates, GrabWise transmits the following minimal information to Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Household Identifiers: A randomly generated household ID, a user-selected household name (e.g., "Our Kitchen"), and a 6-character alphanumeric join code (e.g., GW-XXXXXX).
  • List Items: Item names, quantities, unit types, store tags, brand exactness preferences, and bought/pending statuses.
  • Anonymous Session Identifiers: A Firebase Authentication anonymous unique ID (UID) used strictly to enforce Firestore security rules and prevent unauthorized cross-household access. This UID contains no email address, phone number, hardware serials, or real-world identity.

C. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Backup

GrabWise offers an optional feature allowing users to connect their Google Account for secure cross-device restoration and Google Drive list backups:

  • Data Collected: User's Google email address, display name, and unique authentication ID provided via standard Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Purpose of Collection: Used exclusively to authenticate your identity across your devices, link your household lists, and authenticate authorized Google Drive backup operations.
  • Strict Prohibition on Marketing: We never use your Google account information for promotional communications, third-party profiling, or advertising.

3. Google User Data & API Limited Use Policy

Google API User Data Policy Compliance:
GrabWise's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only request access to basic identity scopes (email and profile) and, if optionally enabled for Drive backup, the specific application-data folder scope (drive.appdata or drive.file).
  • Google user data is never transferred to third parties other than technical infrastructure processors (Google Firebase) required to provide the core synchronization service.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, training generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models, or market research.
  • Human employees or contractors of WiselyRise do not read your private backup files or Google account data unless explicitly requested by you for technical support or required by law.

4. Voice, Camera & Media Handling

A. Voice Grocery Basket (Microphone Dictation)

GrabWise includes a hands-free voice feature allowing you to speak items (e.g., "Tomatoes 1 kg vegetable shop"):

  • Microphone permission is requested solely on-demand when you explicitly tap the microphone button.
  • Audio transcription is processed in real time by your operating system's native speech recognition framework (Android Google Speech Recognition or iOS Speech Framework).
  • WiselyRise Solutions never records, uploads, captures, or retains raw audio recordings on any server. Only the resulting text transcription is staged into your grocery basket.

B. Custom Item Photos & Camera

GrabWise allows you to capture or attach a photo to a grocery item to ensure family members buy the exact variant:

  • Camera and photo gallery access are requested on-demand only when you tap "Take Photo" or "Choose from Gallery".
  • Photos are cropped and compressed directly on your device (target size ≤ 30 KB) before optional upload to Firebase Storage, scoped strictly to your household document.
  • We do not scan your photo library or access metadata (such as EXIF location coordinates).

5. Device Permissions Disclosed

GrabWise requests only the minimum operating system permissions required for user-initiated actions:

Permission Platform Technical Purpose & Necessity
INTERNET Android, Web Required to communicate with Cloud Firestore and Google Authentication when real-time household sync or cloud backup is active.
RECORD_AUDIO Android, iOS Used exclusively on-demand when you tap the microphone button to dictate grocery items. No background listening occurs.
RecognitionService Android Enables querying the device's native on-device speech-to-text recognition service for real-time dictation.
CAMERA (Optional) Android, iOS Requested on-demand only if you choose "Take Photo" to capture a reference picture of a specific grocery product or packaging.

🛡️ Explicit Sensor Restriction: GrabWise does NOT request or access your GPS location, phone contacts, SMS messages, call logs, calendar, biometric hardware, or health sensors.

6. Third-Party Service Providers & Technical Processors

We do not operate our own advertising networks or sell data. We rely exclusively on enterprise-grade cloud service providers as technical data processors:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Issues anonymous session tokens and manages secure Google OAuth login credentials.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Fully managed NoSQL cloud database providing encrypted real-time document sync.
  • Google Cloud Storage: Secure cloud bucket storage for compressed user-attached product photos.
  • Firebase Hosting: Global content delivery network (CDN) hosting the web application (grabwise.web.app).

Google Cloud processes data in compliance with international privacy and security standards, including ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3, and GDPR Data Processing Agreements.

7. Data Retention, Account Erasure & Deletion Requests

We believe in absolute user control over data lifecycle:

A. Automated Lifecycle Cleanups

  • Bought Items: You can configure how long completed items stay visible (Manual, 24 Hours, 48 Hours default, or 7 Days).
  • Stale Households: Cloud households that remain inactive with zero access for over 90 days are automatically flagged for scheduled database purging.

B. In-App Instant Deletion (Self-Service)

You can erase all your data immediately from within the application at any time:

  • Open GrabWise → Tap the Profile & Settings icon (top-right avatar).
  • Scroll to the danger zone and tap "Delete Account & Cloud Data".
  • This action permanently purges your household record, cloud grocery items, invite codes, member associations, and Firebase Authentication UID from Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage. Local data on the device is wiped simultaneously.

C. Web & Email Deletion Request (Right to Erasure)

If you have uninstalled the app or wish to request manual administrative deletion of your cloud records, you can submit an account and data erasure request:

  • Email us at: contact@wiselyrise.in with the subject line "Data Erasure Request - GrabWise".
  • Provide your 6-character Household Code or linked Google account email.
  • Our technical team will permanently wipe your data from our database within 30 days of request verification and provide confirmation.

8. Security & Anti-Brute-Force Safeguards

We enforce rigorous technical safeguards to preserve data integrity and privacy:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile app, web app, and Google Firebase servers are strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Granular Firestore Security Rules: Household documents and item subcollections are strictly locked down to authenticated members of that specific household ID.
  • Brute-Force Protection: To prevent guessing of 6-character invite codes, our backend rate limits attempts and enforces an exponential lockout (5-minute freeze) after 3 consecutive invalid attempts.

9. Children's Privacy Protection

GrabWise is a general-audience household grocery companion and is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at contact@wiselyrise.in, and we will promptly delete the data.
