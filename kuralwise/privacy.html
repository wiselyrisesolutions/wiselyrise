02 · Data Collection

What Information We Collect

✅

We collect ZERO Personal Identifiable Information (PII). No name, no email, no phone number, no account, no password — nothing.

  • No Account Required: KuralWise works out of the box with no registration of any kind.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not access GPS, IP geolocation, or cell tower data.
  • No Audio or Camera Recording: All audio playback occurs locally. The app never accesses your microphone or camera in the current release.
  • No Advertising SDKs: KuralWise contains zero ad networks, zero banner trackers, and zero behavioral profiling scripts or libraries.
  • No Analytics Platform: We do not integrate Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Facebook SDK, or any equivalent analytics service.
03 · Local Storage

On-Device Data Storage

KuralWise stores information locally in a sandboxed SQLite database on your device to power your learning experience. This data is private to the app and your device — it is never transmitted or uploaded anywhere.

  • User Profiles & Preferences: Avatar selection, chosen track (Little Sprouts / School / Exam / Wisdom), font theme, and language mode.
  • Mastery & Spaced Repetition (SM-2): Per-kural review intervals, ease factors, recall scores, correct/incorrect counters, and 8-stage mastery state.
  • Recitation Sprint Records: Personal-best sprint times, average pace per Kural session, and completion counts — stored locally for personal progress tracking only.
  • Daily Streak Records: Current streak and longest streak counts for motivation — no server sync, no leaderboard.
  • Kural Radio Settings: Your preferred daily alarm time and playback speed — stored in local device preferences only.
04 · Children's Privacy

KuralWise features a dedicated Little Sprouts track for young children (ages 3–7) and is designed to be fully compliant with:

  • COPPA — Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (United States)

Specifically, within the children's experience:

  • No push-to-purchase prompts or in-app purchase flows appear in the children's interface.
05 · Device Permissions

App Permissions Explained

KuralWise requests only the minimal permissions strictly required for core functionality:

PermissionWhy It's Needed
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show your local daily Kural Radio alarm notification at your chosen time. The alarm runs entirely on your device's local operating system — no push notification server is involved.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM To deliver your Kural Radio alarm at the exact time you configured (e.g. 7:30 AM). Required on Android 12+ for precise local alarms.
WAKE_LOCK To allow smooth background audio playback of the 28-second Kural Radio capsule when your screen is locked — so the morning ritual completes uninterrupted.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To re-register your daily alarm after a device restart, ensuring your Kural Radio schedule survives a reboot.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or Bluetooth permissions are requested or used.

06 · Third Parties

Third-Party Services

KuralWise does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting SDKs within the app itself.

The only external network interaction is an optional, one-time audio pack download from a static file host (GitHub Releases or Firebase Storage free tier) for users who choose to download additional audio chapter packs. This download is:

  • Entirely optional — the app functions fully offline with the bundled audio corpus.
  • A simple HTTPS file download with no user identification or tracking parameters.
  • Governed by GitHub's or Firebase's own privacy policies for static file serving.

When you share a verse using the system share sheet (e.g. WhatsApp, Messages, Instagram), the content is handled directly by your chosen third-party application under that application's own privacy policy. KuralWise has no visibility into what you share or with whom.

07 · Retention & Deletion

Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides strictly on your device, you maintain complete sovereignty over it:

  • You can reset your learning progress, streak, and SM-2 data at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • Uninstalling KuralWise from your device permanently and irreversibly deletes all local databases, streaks, and preferences.
  • There is no "cloud account" to delete — because we never created one.
