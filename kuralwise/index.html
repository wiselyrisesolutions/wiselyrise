Frequently Asked Questions

Is KuralWise completely free and ad-free?+
Yes — KuralWise is 100% free with zero advertisements, zero in-app purchase prompts, and zero behavioral trackers. The full corpus of 1,330 Kurals, all 4 learning tracks, the full Recitation Championship Suite, and SM-2 memory engine are all included at no cost.
How does SM-2 spaced repetition work for Kurals?+
SM-2 tracks how quickly and accurately you recall each couplet and automatically schedules your next review at the optimal moment before memory decay — 1 day, then 6 days, then progressively longer. Kurals you find easy are reviewed less often; stumble-points are revisited sooner. This is the same algorithm used by Anki and Duolingo.
Can I use KuralWise without an internet connection?+
Absolutely. All 1,330 Kurals, multi-tier meanings, pre-rendered audio capsules, spaced repetition engine, recitation sprint timer, cue-card drills, paced teleprompter, and the situational emotion search all run 100% offline after a single install. No Wi-Fi required — ever.
Is KuralWise suitable for Kural Theni and school competitions?+
Yes — the School Track and Recitation Championship Suite are specifically built for competitions like Kural Theni 2026, Shriram Ilakkiya Kazhagam, and inter-school elocution. Features include a live countdown clock to your target event, Weak Kural Radar for automatic error-cluster diagnosis, a hundredths-precise sprint timer for speed training, and Cue-Card Reflex Drill for judge-trigger practice.
How can teachers or schools use KuralWise?+
KuralWise requires no school server, no login, and no subscription. Teachers direct students to install the free app and select the School Track. Students' readiness rings, Weak Kural Radar reports, and shareable milestone cards give teachers visibility. School Pack is currently in work. Use Get in Touch to join the early-access waitlist and get rollout guidance.
Is the app safe for young children (COPPA compliant)?+
Yes. The Little Sprouts track is designed for ages 3–7 and the app is fully compliant with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy. We collect zero personal information from children. There are no chat rooms, no social feeds, no external web links, and no behavioral advertising anywhere in the app.
What languages does the app support?+
All Thirukkural verses are in classical Tamil with transliteration. Meanings and the app interface are available in both Pure Tamil (நேர்த்தியான தமிழ்) and Tanglish/Bilingual mode, selectable from the language settings or during the initial onboarding setup.
How does the Recitation Sprint timer work?+
The Sprint timer runs a full-screen stopwatch in hundredths-of-a-second precision. Tap anywhere on the screen to mark each Kural complete — no need to look down. The live pace gauge shows your current seconds-per-Kural in real time, color-coded as World Record Pace (≤2.0s), Championship Ready (2.0–3.0s), or Steady Flow (>3.0s). Your best pace and daily streak are logged automatically after each run.
Does KuralWise cover all 1,330 Kurals?+
Yes — the full corpus of all 1,330 Thirukkural couplets across all 3 Paals (Arathuppaal · Porutpaal · Kaamathuppaal) and 133 Adhigarams is included, each with three-tier meanings (kids, standard, scholarly), story context, short moral, thematic tags, and modern life situation keywords.
