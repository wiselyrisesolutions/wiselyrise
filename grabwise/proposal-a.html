FAQ

Got questions?

Is GrabWise really free? Forever? +
Yes, completely free with no hidden fees, no premium tier, no in-app purchases, and absolutely zero ads. GrabWise is our gift to every Indian household that deserves better grocery planning.
Does it work without an internet connection? +
Absolutely. GrabWise is built offline-first — all data is stored locally on your device. Whether you're at a busy market with no signal or in a rural area, the app works perfectly.
Do I need to create an account? +
No account, no sign-up, no email address required. Download the app, open it, and start building your grocery list immediately. We don't collect your personal data.
What languages does GrabWise support? +
GrabWise supports Tamil and English. Every item in the catalog is displayed in both scripts so the whole family — from grandparents to kids — can read and use the list comfortably.
Can I use it on both web and Android? +
Yes! Download the Android app from Google Play for the best experience, or use the Web App at grabwise.web.app on any browser. Both are free.
