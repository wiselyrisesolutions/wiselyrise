The DateWise Story

You remember everything about them. Except the date.

A short story about a late phone call, a lapsed policy, and why we built an app that would rather speak to your heart than your schedule.

Scroll to read ↓
Chapter 01

The 9:00 AM that wasn't a meeting.

It's a Tuesday. You're in the middle of something important-looking. Your phone lights up for half a second, then goes dark again. The reminder arrived exactly on time.

📅
Calendar · nowEvent at 9:00 AM

That's all it said. It didn't say whose event. It didn't say that today your mother turns sixty-two and has been waiting, politely, not to be the one who calls first.

The phone knew the date. It just had no idea what the date meant.

Chapter 02

Three pages that slipped out of the calendar.

Nobody forgets on purpose. Dates slip because the tools we trust were built for a different kind of life, one made of stand-ups, sprints and quarterly reviews.

Amma's birthdayThe call came at 9 PM. She said it was fine. It wasn't.
Your anniversaryA dinner you planned in the car, on the way home.
The insurance renewalA policy that lapsed by exactly one day.

Our calendars are full of meetings. The dates that matter most never had a meeting invite.

Chapter 03

Every app wanted something first.

We went looking for a better place to keep these dates. Everywhere, the same toll booth stood at the door: an email, a password, a permission, a fifteen-second ad.

Create an accountAllow trackingWatch an ad to continueOpen. Type. Done. ✓

We were trying to remember a birthday, not open a bank account. The names of the people you love, their dates, your private notes: that is not data. That is your family. It belongs on your phone and nowhere else.

So we set one rule: DateWise would ask for nothing, and keep everything with you.

Chapter 04

What if your phone said it out loud?

A reminder should do what a good friend does: tell you what is happening and why it matters. So DateWise doesn't just ping. It speaks, in a voice you pick, with a line that actually helps. Choose a moment and listen.
