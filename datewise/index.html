FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Yes. DateWise is free to download and use with all core features — create unlimited reminders, get smart notifications, use the calendar and Inbox, import from contacts, and more. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks Google Drive backup, custom types, unlimited Smart Add, unlimited People Hub entries, and more — no subscription ever.
No account is ever required. DateWise works fully offline. Your data stays on your device. Optional Google Drive backup (Pro) does require a Google account, but that's entirely your choice.
DateWise reads birthday dates stored in your phone contacts and creates reminders automatically. It only reads — never writes or modifies your contacts. You'll see a standard Android permission dialog first.
Yes, completely. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. They fire even if the app is closed, the phone is in airplane mode, or you have no data connection.
DateWise Pro is a one-time in-app purchase (₹49 in India, pricing varies by region). It unlocks Google Drive backup & sync, custom reminder types (beyond the 9 built-in ones), unlimited Smart Add, unlimited People Hub entries, and the Pro stats dashboard. No subscriptions — pay once, keep forever.
Your data never leaves your device unless you enable Google Drive backup. We collect no analytics, run no ads, and share nothing with third parties. The app also supports biometric locking for added security.
With Pro, you can create your own reminder types beyond the 9 built-in ones. Give it a name, pick an emoji from over 120 options, and it appears everywhere in the app — filter bar, add screen, and notifications.
Yes. With Pro, turn on Cloud Backup in Settings on each device using the same Google account. Once it's on, DateWise keeps your reminders in sync automatically in the background — no need to tap anything after the first setup. Manual Backup, Restore, and Merge are still there when you want to sync on demand. See our Sync Guide for the full walkthrough and troubleshooting tips.
Deleted reminders move to the Recycle Bin and stay there safely until you choose to restore them or permanently empty the bin. You can restore any item from Settings → Recycle Bin at any time. One-time reminders whose date has passed also automatically move there when "Auto-Move to Recycle Bin" is turned on in Settings.
Smart Add lets you describe a reminder in plain language — "Mom's birthday next Friday" or speak it aloud — and DateWise auto-detects the date, type, and linked person. Free users get 3 Smart Add trials. Pro users get unlimited Smart Add. You can always create reminders manually for free at any time.
