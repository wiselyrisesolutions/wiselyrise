Reception-to-doctor flow that fits real clinics

Built around how Indian clinics actually work: walk-ins, family visits, queue pressure, and changing contact numbers.

1
Identify patient safely

Search by MRN/phone with deterministic duplicate checks and family grouping without auto-merging clinical identity.

2
Issue token and route

Front desk assigns tokens by practitioner/domain/location with visible queue state for reception and doctor dashboards.

3
Capture consultation essentials

Doctor workflows store complaint, notes, and vitals, while domain payloads handle specialty details without polluting core models.

Privileged Operations
