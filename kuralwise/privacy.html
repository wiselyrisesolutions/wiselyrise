Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 29, 2026 • Last updated: August 29, 2026

The Short Version: KuralWise is built with an uncompromising commitment to user privacy. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize your personal data. The app works 100% offline. All learning progress, bookmarks, custom schedules, and test history remain exclusively on your physical device.

1. Who We Are

KuralWise is developed and maintained by WiselyRise Solutions ("we", "our", or "us"), based in Tamil Nadu, India. Our mission is to build purposeful, privacy-first software that elevates human potential without data surveillance.

Website: https://wiselyrise.in
Contact Email: privacy@wiselyrise.in

2. What Information We Collect

We collect ZERO Personal Identifiable Information (PII).

  • No Account Required: You do not need to provide an email address, phone number, name, or password to use KuralWise.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not access or collect your GPS, IP geolocation, or cell tower data.
  • No Audio or Camera Recording: All audio playback occurs locally on your device. The app never accesses your microphone or camera.
  • No Advertising SDKs: KuralWise contains zero ads, zero banner trackers, and zero behavioral profiling scripts.

3. Local On-Device Data Storage

KuralWise stores information locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device to power your learning experience:

  • User Profiles & Track Preferences: Avatar selection, chosen track (Little Sprouts, School Track, Exam Track, Wisdom Track), and font theme.
  • Mastery & Spaced Repetition (SM-2): Review intervals, recall scores, correct/incorrect counters, and streak records.
  • Kural Radio Alarm Settings: Your preferred notification time and voice speed.

This data is stored purely in your device's sandbox. It is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud.

4. Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

KuralWise features a dedicated "Little Sprouts" track specifically tailored for young children (ages 3–7) and is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play’s Families Policy:

  • We do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • There are no chat rooms, social sharing feeds, or external web links in the child learning interface.
  • No behavioral advertisements or user profiling exist anywhere in the app.

5. Device Permissions

KuralWise requests only the minimal system permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used solely to trigger your local daily morning Kural Radio alarm at your chosen time. These alarms run entirely on your local operating system without connecting to an external notification server.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Allows smooth background audio playback of the 28-second Kural Radio capsules when your screen is locked.

6. Third-Party Services

KuralWise does not integrate any third-party analytics (like Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, or Facebook Pixel) or advertising networks. When you share a verse via the system share sheet, the data is handled directly by your chosen third-party application (e.g. WhatsApp, Messages) under that application's own privacy policy.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides strictly on your device, you maintain complete sovereignty over your data:

  • You can reset your learning progress at any time within the app settings.
  • Uninstalling KuralWise permanently deletes all local databases, streaks, and preferences from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app improvements or legal adjustments. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
