Software is rarely static. Whether you are building from zero or rescuing an existing app from crashes, our services align to your current reality.

Stage 01 · Build

Build from Scratch

We turn validated ideas, operational requirements, and product designs into fast, maintainable, production software. Clean state management, offline-first reliability, and zero bloated frameworks.

What we deliver in Build engagements:

  • Rapid MVPs: Focused initial releases designed to get to the Google Play Store or web without unnecessary delays.
  • Custom Business Tools: Digitize paper/Excel workflows with role-based internal portals and dashboards.
  • Offline-First Architecture: Resilient local databases (SQLite/SQLCipher) that work reliably without active internet.
Stage 02 · Test

App Quality Assurance

Real human exploratory testing across real Android and iOS devices. We uncover subtle edge-case bugs, race conditions, memory leaks, and input quirks before public release.

View Health Check Packages →

Our QA testing scope includes:

  • Critical Journey Testing: End-to-end verification of onboarding, payment, booking, and authentication flows.
  • Network & Lifecycle Drops: Testing abrupt connection drops, airplane mode toggles, and background resume stability.
  • Actionable Deliverables: Clean defect report classifying bugs by severity (Critical, High, Medium, Low) with exact reproduction steps.
Stage 03 · Fix

App Rescue & Repair

Inherited a broken codebase? Previous developers left mid-project? We analyze existing Flutter and Android repositories, isolate the root cause of crashes, and resolve them without an expensive rewrite.

Common App Rescue scenarios we solve:

  • Fatal Exceptions & ANRs: Profiling and eliminating memory leaks, null pointer crashes, and UI freezes.
  • Firebase & Auth Failures: Fixing broken Google Sign-In, Phone Auth OTP loops, and Firestore rule violations.
  • Play Store Rejections: Bringing outdated Target SDK versions, privacy disclosures, and app bundles into full compliance.
Stage 04 · Improve

Continuous Evolution

Your product works, but users are requesting features or encountering UX friction. We provide ongoing engineering maintenance, performance optimization, and deliberate feature rollouts.

How we improve live software:

  • UI/UX Polish: Eliminating clunky animations, fixing touch target sizes, and streamlining user journeys.
  • Feature Sprints: Scoping and shipping specific high-priority modules with zero disruption to active users.
  • Version Upgrades: Keeping dependencies, Flutter SDKs, and platform APIs current with latest mobile OS standards.

Client FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you require access to source code for an App Health Check?

No. For pre-launch QA, we only need an installable build (APK / Android App Bundle or iOS TestFlight invite) and test credentials. We do not require source code access unless you also want code-level triage.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing app builds or requirements?

Absolutely. We treat all client concepts, builds, and proprietary business workflows with absolute confidentiality. We are happy to sign standard non-disclosure agreements before review.

What technologies does WiselyRise specialize in?

Our core mobile specialty is Flutter & Dart for cross-platform apps, plus native Android (Kotlin). On web and backend, we build with clean HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript, REST APIs, Firebase, and SQLite/SQLCipher on-device databases.

How does payment and billing work?

For fixed-scope services like the App Health Check, payment is straightforward upfront. For custom build and rescue projects, we work in milestone-based sprints with clear deliverables before each invoice.
